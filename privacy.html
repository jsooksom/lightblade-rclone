Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Purpose and access

LightBlade rclone Backup accesses only the owner's Google Drive after explicit OAuth consent. Access is used solely to synchronize backup data selected and configured by the owner.

Data handling

The application does not collect, sell, share, advertise against, or analyze Google user data. It has no analytics, cookies, forms, trackers, or third-party application backend.

Credential storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored only on the owner's LightBlade host. They are not stored in this public website or its source repository.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from Google Account third-party connections.
